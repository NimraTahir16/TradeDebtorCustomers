<h1>Trade Debtor Customers </h1>

 

<h2>Description</h2>
 Trade Debtor Customers <br>

<h4>Project Overview</h4>

This project is an interactive Power BI report designed to analyze customer revenue concentration and monitor accounts receivable aging. It helps finance and credit control teams track top-performing customers, evaluate sales trends over time, and manage cash flow risk by pinpointing overdue receivables. By combining commercial sales metrics with debtor aging analysis on a single screen, the dashboard enables teams to make strategic credit decisions and maintain healthy working capital.

<h4>Key Features</h4>

Tracks overall sales revenue, invoice counts, and growth comparisons against previous periods to gauge commercial performance.  
Measures top customer revenue concentration and compares their combined contribution against the rest of the customer base.  
Ranks top client accounts with visual data bars displaying their individual share of total sales and order volumes.  
Visualizes monthly revenue trends over time, tracking key accounts against aggregate sales trajectories. 
Categorizes total outstanding receivables into standard aging intervals (Current, 30 Days, 60 Days, and 90+ Days) to monitor liquidity and credit exposure.  
Breaks down customer-wise outstanding debt distribution using a proportional share visual.  
Provides a granular customer aging matrix detailing overdue balances across every aging bracket to support collections prioritization.  
Includes dynamic slicers for date ranges, customer accounts, geographic regions, and customer categories to enable targeted filtering. 


Business Value: Provided a clear overview of sales performance and helped identify growth opportunities, high-performing products, and areas for improvement.
